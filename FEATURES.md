# RealVolt Feature Map

## Sellable SaaS plans
### CRM
CRM, Pipeline, Clients, Open Houses, Forms, Property Pages, Automations, Integrations.

### Team
CRM plan + shared leads, assignments and team collaboration.

### Brokerage
Full platform + Transactions, Back Office, Trust & Accounting, Agent onboarding, commission plans, payouts, audit, reports, E-Sign and year-end records.

## Role model
Realtor: own CRM, transactions, documents, earnings, statements, expenses, property pages.
Brokerage staff: brokerage-wide operations according to role.
Back Office/Trust modules: hidden and blocked for ordinary Realtors.
Platform admin: server-side/service-role tooling only.

## Realtor profile
Name, photo, bio, phone, email, website, social links, brokerage branding, licence data, GST data, preferences.
Profile data can populate property pages and other marketing.

## Property Page Builder
Create from listing, templates, gallery, video, floor plan, open-house info, Realtor profile, contact form, QR, analytics, default RealVolt URL, custom-domain architecture.

## Back Office
Realtor invitations, compensation plans/history, transaction review, document deficiencies, CRI/lawyer workflow, trust deposit verification, 5% GST, release approval, payout, reconciliation, T4A/year-end records, audit and close/lock.

## Agent accounting
Deal-by-deal commission statement, monthly/yearly expenses, paid commissions, balances, tax documents, future online payment integration.

## Integrations
Email provider, Meta lead webhooks, Google architecture, website form APIs. External credentials still required.
